Bank account transfer application

Use this to ask your bank to move your account from one branch to another branch of the same bank, for example after you shift to a new city.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Branch Manager, [Bank Name], [Branch Name] Date: [Date] Subject: Request to transfer my account to [New Branch Name] branch Respected Sir/Madam, I hold a savings account, number [Account Number], in your branch. I have shifted to [New City or Area] because [Reason, for example of a job transfer], and it is now difficult for me to visit this branch. I request you to kindly transfer my account to your [New Branch Name] branch, [New Branch Address or Code]. My new address is: [New Address] Thanking you, Yours faithfully, [Your Name] Account No.: [Account Number] Mobile: [Mobile Number] Signature: ____________
सेवा में, शाखा प्रबंधक महोदय, [बैंक का नाम], [शाखा का नाम] दिनांक: [दिनांक] विषय: खाता [नई शाखा का नाम] शाखा में स्थानांतरित करने हेतु आवेदन महोदय, निवेदन है कि आपकी शाखा में मेरा बचत खाता है, जिसकी संख्या [खाता संख्या] है। [कारण, जैसे नौकरी में तबादला होने] के कारण मैं अब [नया शहर या क्षेत्र] में रहने लगा/लगी हूँ। इसलिए इस शाखा में आना मेरे लिए कठिन हो गया है। अतः आपसे अनुरोध है कि मेरा खाता [नई शाखा का नाम] शाखा में स्थानांतरित करने की कृपा करें। मेरा नया पता इस प्रकार है: [नया पता] धन्यवाद। भवदीय, [आपका नाम] खाता संख्या: [खाता संख्या] मोबाइल: [मोबाइल नंबर] हस्ताक्षर: ____________

Before you send it

  • Many banks let you change the home branch in net banking or the app. Check there first.
  • Your account number normally stays the same within a bank. The branch code (IFSC) changes, so update it wherever you receive money, such as salary or refunds.
  • Attach proof of the new address if you want the address changed at the same time.
  • Return or keep unused cheque leaves as the branch tells you. Old leaves may carry the old IFSC.

What to attach

  • Passbook
  • Proof of new address, if the address is also changing
  • Identity proof

Common questions

Can I give this letter at the new branch?

Often yes. Many banks accept the request at either branch. Ask the branch that is easier for you to visit.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the bank has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
