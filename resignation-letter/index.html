Resignation letter

Use this to resign from your job in a polite, professional way. It states your last working day and offers a proper handover.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Manager, [Company or Office Name], [City] Date: [Date] Subject: Resignation from the post of [Your Designation] Dear Sir/Madam, Please accept this letter as my resignation from the post of [Your Designation] at [Company or Office Name]. As per my notice period of [Notice Period], my last working day will be [Last Working Day]. I have decided to leave because [Reason, for example I have received an opportunity that fits my long-term plans]. This was not an easy decision. I am grateful for the guidance and support I received here. During the notice period I will complete my current work and hand over my duties properly. Please let me know how I can make the change easier for the team. Thank you for everything. Yours sincerely, [Your Name] [Your Designation] Employee ID: [Employee ID]

Before you send it

  • Read your appointment letter for the notice period before you write the date. The last working day should match it.
  • Tell your manager in person before you send the letter. No one likes to learn it from an email.
  • Keep the reason short and positive. A resignation letter stays in your file; it is not the place for complaints.
  • Ask HR in writing for your relieving letter, experience certificate and full and final settlement.

Common questions

Do I have to give a reason for resigning?

No. A reason is a courtesy. You can simply write that you are resigning for personal reasons.

Can I resign by email?

In most companies, yes. Send it to your manager with HR in copy, and keep a copy for yourself.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the company has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
