Joining letter

Use this on your first day in a new job, or when you return after long leave, to report in writing that you have joined duty.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The HR Manager, [Company or Office Name], [City] Date: [Date] Subject: Joining report Dear Sir/Madam, With reference to your appointment letter number [Reference Number] dated [Date of Appointment Letter], I accept the post of [Your Designation] on the terms stated in it. I am reporting for duty today, [Date of Joining], in the [forenoon/afternoon]. I am submitting copies of my documents as listed in the appointment letter. I thank you for this opportunity and assure you of my sincere work. Yours sincerely, [Your Name] Mobile: [Mobile Number]

Before you send it

  • Quote the number and date of your appointment letter. It ties your joining to the offer.
  • Government and many private offices record whether you joined in the forenoon or afternoon. Keep that line.
  • Hand it in and ask for a signed copy for your own file.

What to attach

  • Copies of the documents listed in the appointment letter
  • Passport-size photographs, if asked

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the company has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
