Leave application for your own marriage

Use this to apply for leave from work for your own wedding.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Manager, [Company or Office Name], [City] Date: [Date] Subject: Leave for my marriage from [From Date] to [To Date] Dear Sir/Madam, I am happy to share that my marriage is fixed for [Wedding Date] at [Place]. I will need time for the preparations and the ceremonies. I request you to kindly grant me leave for [Number of Days] days, from [From Date] to [To Date]. I will hand over my current work to [Name of Colleague] before I leave and will share a short handover note. I will rejoin on [Date of Rejoining]. It would be a pleasure to see you at the wedding. Thank you. Yours sincerely, [Your Name] [Your Designation] Employee ID: [Employee ID]

When to use this letter

Use this to ask for a longer stretch of leave around your wedding. Since the dates are known well ahead, managers expect the request early and with a plan for your work. Some employers have a separate marriage leave; in others it is taken from earned leave. Your HR policy will say which.

Before you send it

  • Apply several weeks ahead. Long leave needs planning by your team.
  • Offer a written handover: what is pending, where the files are, whom to call.
  • If your leave balance is short, say which part you request as leave without pay.

How to send or submit it

Speak to your manager first, then send the application by email or the HR portal so that there is a record. Share your handover note with the team before your last working day, and set an out-of-office reply with the name of the person covering for you.

Mistakes that get it sent back

  • Applied for only a few days before the wedding.
  • The dates of leave and the date of rejoining are not stated exactly.
  • No handover plan for work that will fall due while you are away.
  • The leave asked for is more than your balance, with nothing said about the extra days.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the company has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
