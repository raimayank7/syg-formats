Request letter for experience certificate

Use this to ask your present or former employer for an experience certificate.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The HR Manager, [Company or Office Name], [City] Date: [Date] Subject: Request for an experience certificate Dear Sir/Madam, I worked with [Company or Office Name] as [Your Designation] in the [Department] department from [Date of Joining] to [Last Working Day]. My employee ID was [Employee ID]. I need an experience certificate for [Purpose, for example my new employer's joining formalities]. I request you to kindly issue it, stating my designation and my period of service. You may send it to my email, [Email Address], or I can collect it from the office. Thank you. Yours sincerely, [Your Name] Mobile: [Mobile Number]

Before you send it

  • Give your employee ID and exact dates. HR checks them against the records.
  • Say what the certificate should mention: designation, period, and the nature of work if you need it.
  • If you left recently, check that your full and final settlement is complete. Many companies issue the certificate with it.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the company has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
