Application for name change in electricity bill

Use this to ask the electricity office to transfer a connection to your name, for example after you buy a house or after the death of the earlier holder.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Sub-Divisional Officer, [Name of Electricity Company or Board], [Sub-division or Area] Date: [Date] Subject: Request to change the name on electricity connection number [Consumer Number] Sir/Madam, The electricity connection number [Consumer Number] at [Address of Connection] is at present in the name of [Present Name on Bill]. I request you to transfer this connection to my name because [Reason, for example I purchased this house on 10 March 2026 / the holder, my father, passed away on 2 January 2026]. All bills up to [Month of Last Paid Bill] have been paid. I am attaching copies of the papers listed below and am ready to complete any form and pay any fee required. Yours faithfully, [Your Name] Mobile: [Mobile Number]

Before you send it

  • Every electricity company has its own form and document list for a change of name. Collect it from the office or its website; this letter goes with it.
  • Clear all pending bills first. The transfer is not processed with dues outstanding.
  • For a transfer after a death, the office usually asks for the death certificate and proof that you are the legal heir, or consent from the other heirs. Ask what it accepts.

What to attach

  • Copy of the latest paid bill
  • Proof of ownership or occupation (for example the sale deed or rent agreement)
  • Identity proof
  • Death certificate of the earlier holder, where that is the reason

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the office has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
