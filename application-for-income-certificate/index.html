Application for income certificate

Use this as a covering application to the tehsildar or the office that issues income certificates in your area, where a plain application is accepted.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Tehsildar, [Tehsil or Taluk Name], [District] Date: [Date] Subject: Application for an income certificate Sir/Madam, I am [Your Name], [son/daughter/wife] of [Father's or Husband's Name], a resident of [Your Address]. I need an income certificate for [Purpose, for example my daughter's scholarship form]. The details of my family's income are: Occupation of the earning member: [Occupation] Annual income from all sources: Rs [Annual Income] Number of family members: [Number of Family Members] I declare that this information is true to the best of my knowledge. I request you to kindly issue the income certificate. Copies of the required documents are attached. Yours faithfully, [Your Name] Mobile: [Mobile Number]
सेवा में, तहसीलदार महोदय, [तहसील का नाम], [ज़िला] दिनांक: [दिनांक] विषय: आय प्रमाण पत्र जारी करने हेतु आवेदन महोदय, निवेदन है कि मैं [आपका नाम], [पिता या पति का नाम] का पुत्र/पुत्री/पत्नी, [आपका पता] का निवासी हूँ। मुझे [उद्देश्य, जैसे बेटी की छात्रवृत्ति के फ़ॉर्म] के लिए आय प्रमाण पत्र की आवश्यकता है। मेरे परिवार की आय का विवरण इस प्रकार है: कमाने वाले सदस्य का व्यवसाय: [व्यवसाय] सभी स्रोतों से वार्षिक आय: [वार्षिक आय] रुपये परिवार के सदस्यों की संख्या: [परिवार के सदस्यों की संख्या] मैं घोषणा करता/करती हूँ कि यह जानकारी मेरी जानकारी के अनुसार सत्य है। कृपया आय प्रमाण पत्र जारी करने की कृपा करें। आवश्यक कागज़ों की प्रतियाँ साथ में लगी हैं। भवदीय, [आपका नाम] मोबाइल: [मोबाइल नंबर]

When to use this letter

An income certificate is asked for when you apply for scholarships, fee concessions, some welfare schemes and reservations in admission. It is issued by the revenue authority of your area. Most states now take applications online or at service centres, so use this letter only where a written application is still accepted or asked for.

Before you send it

  • In most states, income, caste and residence certificates are now applied for online on the state's e-District or service portal, or at a common service centre, on a fixed form. Check your state's portal before you write a letter.
  • The document list differs by state. Ask the office or see the portal; do not rely on a general list.
  • State the income honestly. A false declaration to a public office is an offence.
  • The same layout works for a residence (domicile) certificate: change the subject and replace the income details with how long you have lived at the address.

What to attach

  • Identity and address proof
  • Proof of income, such as a salary slip, as your state's form requires
  • Self-declaration, if the form asks for one

How to send or submit it

Submit it at the tehsil or taluk office, or the office your state names, with the documents on its list, or apply on the state portal. Keep the acknowledgement slip and use its number to check the status.

Mistakes that get it sent back

  • The income declared does not match the proof attached.
  • The documents that your state's list requires are missing.
  • It is applied for in an area where you do not live.
  • The state's prescribed form or online process was skipped.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the office has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
