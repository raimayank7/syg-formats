Society meeting notice format

Use this to call a general meeting of a housing society or residents' association, with the date, place and agenda.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

[NAME OF HOUSING SOCIETY OR ASSOCIATION] [Address] NOTICE OF GENERAL BODY MEETING Date: [Date] All members are informed that a general body meeting of the society will be held as below: Day and date: [Day and Date of Meeting] Time: [Time] Place: [Venue] Agenda: 1. [Agenda Item 1, for example to read and confirm the minutes of the last meeting] 2. [Agenda Item 2, for example to approve the accounts for the year 2025-26] 3. [Agenda Item 3, for example to decide on repainting the building] 4. Any other matter with the permission of the chair. All members are requested to attend on time. Members who wish to raise a point may give it in writing to the secretary by [Last Date for Points]. By order of the managing committee [Name of Secretary] Secretary

Before you send it

  • Check your society's bye-laws for how many days' notice a general meeting needs and how the notice must be delivered. The bye-laws, and your state's law for societies, come first.
  • List every matter that needs a decision. Decisions on matters not on the agenda are often challenged later.
  • Put the notice on the notice board and also send it to every member in the way the bye-laws say.
  • Keep a signed copy with the date it was displayed.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the school or society has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
