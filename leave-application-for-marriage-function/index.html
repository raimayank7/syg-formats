Leave application for a marriage in the family

Use this to ask the school for leave to attend a wedding or another family function. It works for school and college students.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Principal, [School Name], [City] Date: [Date] Subject: Application for leave to attend a family wedding Respected Sir/Madam, I am [Your Name], a student of class [Class and Section]. The wedding of my [Relation, for example elder sister] will take place on [Wedding Date] at [Place]. My family and I have to be there for the ceremonies. I request you to kindly grant me leave for [Number of Days] days, from [From Date] to [To Date]. I will collect the notes from my classmates and complete all pending work when I return. Thanking you, Yours obediently, [Your Name] Class: [Class and Section] Roll No.: [Roll Number]
सेवा में, प्रधानाचार्य महोदय/महोदया, [विद्यालय का नाम], [शहर] दिनांक: [दिनांक] विषय: परिवार में विवाह के कारण अवकाश हेतु प्रार्थना पत्र महोदय/महोदया, सविनय निवेदन है कि मैं [आपका नाम], कक्षा [कक्षा और वर्ग] का छात्र/छात्रा हूँ। मेरे [संबंध, जैसे बड़े भाई] का विवाह [विवाह की तारीख] को [स्थान] में होना तय हुआ है। इसमें मेरा उपस्थित रहना आवश्यक है। अतः आपसे प्रार्थना है कि मुझे [किस तारीख से] से [किस तारीख तक] तक [दिनों की संख्या] दिनों का अवकाश देने की कृपा करें। लौटकर मैं अपना छूटा हुआ कार्य पूरा कर लूँगा/लूँगी। धन्यवाद। आपका आज्ञाकारी शिष्य/शिष्या, [आपका नाम] कक्षा: [कक्षा और वर्ग] अनुक्रमांक: [अनुक्रमांक]

Before you send it

  • Apply at least a few days before the leave starts. Planned leave should never be a surprise.
  • Say whose wedding it is. Schools give leave more easily for a close relative.
  • Do not ask for leave during exams or tests unless there is no other way. If dates clash, say so and ask what can be done.
  • A parent's signature or a copy of the wedding card helps if the leave is longer than three days.

What to attach

  • Copy of the wedding card (optional, for longer leave)

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the school or college has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
