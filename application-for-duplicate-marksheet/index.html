Application for duplicate marksheet

Use this to ask your school, college or university for a duplicate marksheet when the original is lost or damaged.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Principal, [School or College Name], [City] Date: [Date] Subject: Request for a duplicate marksheet of [Class or Course], [Year of Passing] Respected Sir/Madam, I passed [Class or Course] from your institution in [Year of Passing]. My roll number was [Roll Number]. My original marksheet has been [lost/damaged] [How, for example while shifting house]. I need it for [Purpose, for example a job application]. I request you to kindly issue a duplicate marksheet, or guide me on the steps and fee for it. I am attaching the papers listed below. Thanking you, Yours faithfully, [Your Name] Father's Name: [Father's Name] Mobile: [Mobile Number]

Before you send it

  • For board examinations (class 10 and 12), the duplicate is issued by the board, not by the school. The school can forward your request or tell you the board's process.
  • Many boards and universities ask for a police report or a newspaper notice for a lost marksheet. Ask before you apply.
  • Keep a photocopy or a phone photo of the lost marksheet with the application if you have one. It gives the office every number it needs.

What to attach

  • Photocopy of the lost marksheet, if you have one
  • Copy of the police report for a lost document, if required
  • Identity proof

Common questions

How long does a duplicate marksheet take?

It depends on the board or university. Ask the office when you apply; do not rely on a fixed number of days from any website.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the school or college has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
