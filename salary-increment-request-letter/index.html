Salary increment request letter

Use this to ask your manager for a salary increase, with reasons based on your work.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Manager, [Company or Office Name], [City] Date: [Date] Subject: Request for a review of my salary Dear Sir/Madam, I have been working as [Your Designation] since [Date of Joining]. In this time I have taken on more responsibility, including [New Responsibilities]. Some results of my work in the past year: - [Result 1, for example reduced billing errors by cross-checking every invoice] - [Result 2] - [Result 3] My salary has not been revised since [Date of Last Revision]. In view of my work and added duties, I request you to kindly review my salary. I would be glad to discuss this at a time that suits you. Thank you for considering my request. Yours sincerely, [Your Name] [Your Designation] Employee ID: [Employee ID]

Before you send it

  • Lead with results, not needs. "Prices have gone up" is true for everyone; your results are yours alone.
  • Use numbers where you honestly can: work completed, time saved, customers handled.
  • Choose your moment: after a good result or at appraisal time, not during a crisis.
  • Ask for a meeting. A raise is usually agreed in a conversation, and the letter opens it.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the company has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
