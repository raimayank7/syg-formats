Application for change of section

Use this to ask the principal to move you to another section of the same class, for example because of timing, distance or a subject choice.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Principal, [School Name], [City] Date: [Date] Subject: Request for change of section Respected Sir/Madam, I am [Your Name], a student of class [Class], section [Present Section], roll number [Roll Number]. I request you to kindly shift me to section [Section Wanted] because [Reason, for example my optional subject is taught in that section]. This change will help me to study better and attend all my classes without difficulty. I promise to follow all the rules of the new section. I shall be thankful to you for this kindness. Yours obediently, [Your Name] Class: [Class] [Present Section]

Before you send it

  • Give a study reason: a subject, a timetable clash or a real difficulty. "My friends are there" is not a reason a principal can accept.
  • Apply early in the session. A change after the first term is rarely allowed.
  • A parent's signature makes the request stronger.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the school or college has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
