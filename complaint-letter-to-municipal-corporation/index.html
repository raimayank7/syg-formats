Complaint letter to municipal corporation

Use this to complain to the municipal corporation, council or panchayat about a local problem: garbage, a blocked drain, broken street lights, a damaged road or water supply.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Commissioner / Executive Officer, [Name of Municipal Corporation or Council], [City] Date: [Date] Subject: Complaint about [Problem in Short, for example street lights not working] in [Area or Ward] Sir/Madam, I am a resident of [Your Address], ward number [Ward Number]. I wish to bring the following problem to your notice. [Describe the Problem, for example all six street lights on our lane, from the temple to the main road, have not worked for three weeks]. Because of this, [Effect on Residents, for example the lane is fully dark after 6 pm and women and children feel unsafe]. The problem began around [Date the Problem Began]. I request you to kindly have it attended to at the earliest. The residents of the area will be thankful. Yours faithfully, [Your Name] Mobile: [Mobile Number] Other residents who join this complaint: 1. [Name and House Number] 2. [Name and House Number]
सेवा में, आयुक्त / अधिशासी अधिकारी महोदय, [नगर निगम या नगर पालिका का नाम], [शहर] दिनांक: [दिनांक] विषय: [क्षेत्र या वार्ड] में [समस्या संक्षेप में, जैसे स्ट्रीट लाइट खराब होने] के संबंध में शिकायत महोदय, निवेदन है कि मैं [आपका पता], वार्ड संख्या [वार्ड संख्या] का निवासी हूँ। मैं आपका ध्यान निम्नलिखित समस्या की ओर दिलाना चाहता/चाहती हूँ। [समस्या का विवरण]। इसके कारण [निवासियों को होने वाली परेशानी]। यह समस्या लगभग [समस्या शुरू होने की तारीख] से है। अतः आपसे प्रार्थना है कि इस समस्या का जल्द से जल्द समाधान करवाने की कृपा करें। क्षेत्र के सभी निवासी आपके आभारी रहेंगे। भवदीय, [आपका नाम] मोबाइल: [मोबाइल नंबर]

When to use this letter

Use this for problems in public spaces that the local body looks after: garbage not collected, drains overflowing, street lights off, potholes, stray cattle, water logging. First find out which body covers your area, whether a municipal corporation, a council, a town or a gram panchayat, and which ward or zone you are in.

Before you send it

  • Say exactly where: lane, landmark, pole number or house numbers. A worker must be able to find the spot from your letter.
  • One problem per letter. A letter about lights, drains and dogs together gets passed around and solved by no one.
  • Signatures of a few neighbours make a complaint harder to ignore.
  • Many cities also take complaints on an app, website or helpline. Register it there too and note the complaint number.
  • Keep a stamped copy. If nothing happens, write again to the same officer and give the date of this letter.

What to attach

  • Photos of the problem, with the date
  • Copy of earlier complaints, if any

How to send or submit it

Give it at the ward or zonal office and get a receiving stamp, and register the same complaint on the city's app, portal or helpline if there is one. A copy to your ward councillor often speeds things up.

Mistakes that get it sent back

  • The exact spot is not identified.
  • Sent to the wrong department or the wrong ward.
  • No name and contact number, so staff cannot confirm the place or report back.
  • A problem on private property, which the local body does not handle.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the office has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
