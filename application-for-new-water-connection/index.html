Application for a new water connection

Use this as a covering letter to the water supply office when you apply for a new household water connection.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Executive Engineer, [Water Supply Department or Board], [City] Date: [Date] Subject: Application for a new domestic water connection Sir/Madam, I am the [owner/tenant] of the house at [Address of the House], ward number [Ward Number]. The house does not have a water connection at present. I request you to kindly sanction a new domestic water connection for this house. There are [Number of Family Members] members in my family. The nearest supply line runs along [Nearest Road or Lane]. I am attaching copies of the documents listed below. I am ready to fill in the prescribed form and to pay the connection charges and deposit as per the rules. Yours faithfully, [Your Name] Mobile: [Mobile Number]

Before you send it

  • Water boards have a prescribed form and fee. This letter goes with the form; it does not replace it.
  • Tenants are usually asked for the owner's written consent. Attach it if you rent the house.
  • Ask whether the pipe work from the main line to your house is done by the department or by a licensed plumber whom you must hire.

What to attach

  • Proof of ownership (or rent agreement and the owner's consent)
  • Identity proof
  • Latest property tax receipt, if asked

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the office has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
