Leave application for urgent work at home

Use this for one or two days of leave when you have to travel or help with urgent work at home. It is the short, all-purpose school leave letter.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To The Class Teacher, [School Name], [City] Date: [Date] Subject: Application for leave for [Number of Days] day(s) Respected Sir/Madam, I am [Your Name] of class [Class and Section]. I have to [Reason, for example go to my village with my parents] on [Date of Leave]. So I will not be able to come to school on that day. Kindly grant me leave for [Number of Days] day(s). I will be back in class on [Date of Return]. Thanking you, Yours obediently, [Your Name] Roll No.: [Roll Number]
सेवा में, कक्षा अध्यापक महोदय/महोदया, [विद्यालय का नाम], [शहर] दिनांक: [दिनांक] विषय: [दिनों की संख्या] दिन के अवकाश हेतु प्रार्थना पत्र महोदय/महोदया, सविनय निवेदन है कि मैं [आपका नाम], कक्षा [कक्षा और वर्ग] का छात्र/छात्रा हूँ। मुझे [अवकाश की तारीख] को [कारण, जैसे अपने माता-पिता के साथ गाँव जाना] है। इसलिए मैं उस दिन विद्यालय नहीं आ सकूँगा/सकूँगी। कृपया मुझे [दिनों की संख्या] दिन का अवकाश प्रदान करें। मैं [लौटने की तारीख] को कक्षा में उपस्थित हो जाऊँगा/जाऊँगी। धन्यवाद। आपका आज्ञाकारी शिष्य/शिष्या, [आपका नाम] अनुक्रमांक: [अनुक्रमांक]

Before you send it

  • "Urgent piece of work" alone tells the teacher nothing. Add a few plain words about what the work is.
  • Mention the day you will return, so the teacher knows the leave has an end.
  • For a single day, four or five lines are enough. Do not stretch it.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the school or college has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
