Notice to landlord to vacate the house

Use this when you are a tenant and want to tell your landlord, in writing, that you will leave the rented house on a given date.

Tap a yellow blank and type your own details. What you type stays in your browser only.

To [Landlord's Name], [Landlord's Address] Date: [Date] Subject: Notice to vacate the house at [Address of the Rented House] Dear Sir/Madam, I have been your tenant at the above address since [Date Tenancy Began]. I wish to inform you that I will vacate the house on [Date of Vacating], because [Reason, for example I am shifting to another city for work]. Please treat this letter as the notice of [Notice Period, for example one month] required under our rent agreement. I will pay the rent and the electricity and water bills up to that date, and will hand over the keys after you have seen the house. I request you to kindly return my security deposit of Rs [Deposit Amount] on the day I hand over the keys, after adjusting any dues as per the agreement. Thank you for your cooperation during my stay. Yours sincerely, [Tenant's Name] Mobile: [Mobile Number]

Before you send it

  • Read the rent agreement first. It fixes the notice period and what can be deducted from the deposit.
  • Give the notice in a way you can prove later: by hand with a signed copy, by registered post, or by email or WhatsApp with an acknowledgement.
  • Take dated photos of the house before you hand over the keys.
  • Ask for a simple note from the landlord that the house is handed over and no dues remain.

This is a general format, written by us, to save you time. If the office concerned has its own printed form, use that form. Last checked: 11 November 2026.
