Formats

About Formats

Formats is a free collection of application, letter, declaration and notice formats for school, college, bank, office, government offices and home. You fill in the blanks on the page, then copy, print, download or send the letter. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Where the content comes from

  • Every letter, tip and guide on this site is our own original writing. Nothing is copied from books, other websites or official forms.
  • The letters follow the usual Indian layout of a formal application: receiver, date, subject, greeting, body, thanks, name and signature.
  • Where a page mentions a law or rule (for example the Right to Information Act, 2005), it names the law and says when we last checked it. Rules, fees and time limits change and differ from state to state, so always confirm them with the office concerned. Facts on this site were last checked on 11 November 2026.
  • Declarations and affidavit drafts are general wording only. An office, court or notary may ask for its own wording, stamp paper or attestation.

What this site is not

It is not a government site, not legal advice and not connected with any school, bank or office. Official forms, where an office has them, come first.
